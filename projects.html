Research and Formal Writing

Research output 1 · Legal analysis

Authored a formal research paper analyzing Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo, the 2024 Supreme Court decision overturning the Chevron deference doctrine. Argued that the ruling correctly reestablished the judiciary's constitutional duty to interpret statutes, limiting executive agency overreach, and examined the implications for the SEC, EPA, FCC, and administrative law more broadly.

Research output 2 · Macroeconomic legal update

Co-authored Legal Update 6 (December 2024), covering housing market dynamics including the new-home premium falling to 3%, its lowest level since 1980; Federal Reserve rate policy with a projected cut to 4.25% to 4.5%; commodity market impacts of proposed tariffs; the Biden pardon; the South Korean martial law declaration; and the FTC antitrust investigation into Microsoft.
